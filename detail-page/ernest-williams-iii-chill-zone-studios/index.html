About

Chill Zone Studios LLC creates cinematic AI films, music videos and branded commercials with 90s soul. Remote across the US from Indiana. We're an independent production studio built on original storytelling. The characters stay consistent from episode to episode, the music is part of the story, and the work is finished like film — concept, creative direction, generation, edit, sound, and delivery under one roof. The AI is our production pipeline. The storytelling is the product. What we produce: - Original films & episodic series - Commercials & branded content - Music videos - Animation - Character-consistent content across episodes and campaigns Proof of work: Ghost-Writer, our original Southern Gothic supernatural series set in 1998 New Orleans — three completed episodes, 44:43 total. Episode 1 is an Official Selection of the Black AI Film Festival Reel Sessions 2026 and the NOMOREFROG AI Film Awards 2026; Episode 2 was a Semi-Finalist at the Urban Short Film Festival 2026 (Chicago). Alongside it run Live with Lexi, our recurring AI-hosted entertainment series, and commercial concept work for fashion and service brands. Who hires us: Producers, agencies, and brands who need cinematic content delivered end-to-end without a traditional shoot's overhead — and filmmakers who need a production partner fluent in AI workflows.
