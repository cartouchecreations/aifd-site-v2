About

Talentless AI is an AI-native production studio in Austin, Texas. It was founded by Steve Mudd, a filmmaker who wrote, produced and directed the feature film Seclusion before working in brand storytelling at Ogilvy during the IBM Watson era and leading global messaging and content at NetApp. He won the 2026 AI Leadership Award from the Austin AI Alliance. The studio works in three lanes. Brand and live events. Cinematic opening films, campaign video and guest activations for DTN, Hilton, Marriott, YPO, Texas Realtors, Encore Global and Austin's Pizza. Recent work includes a leadership summit film built from AI VFX layered over live-action plates, and an explainer for Peak Nano that cross-sections a nanolayered metamaterial measured in nanometers, a product with no set to build and nothing to point a camera at. Media programs. Always-on publishing operations. FutureKeepers ships advocacy video every week in eight Southeast Asian languages, produced natively in each rather than subtitled. Radical Discourse puts historical figures in full-performance rap battles and has 93,700 subscribers. LINQ, a K-12 software platform, got a brand character named Q and an ongoing content operation around him. Narrative. AI VFX, previz, digital doubles and production support for film and television, plus Backlot74, the studio's own slate of six original microdrama series in development. Originals include Threshold , a short film with 111,000 views, and The Painter , a two-and-a-half-minute continuous shot that would be close to impossible to capture in real life. The studio is deliberately model-agnostic. Seedance, Kling, Gemini Omni Flash, MiniMax H3, Luma Ray and Runway all get cast per shot, the way a director casts an actor, because every model has a different strength and standardising on one means bending the work to fit the tool. Everything is generated as coverage against a script and a shot list, so roughly 80% of what comes back goes into the edit, and human editors and directors choose the takes and finish the picture.
