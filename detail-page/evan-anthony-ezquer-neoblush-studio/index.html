About

Neoblush Studio creates story-first AI films, cinematic microdramas, and social media ads for brands, producers, and creators worldwide. Founded by AI filmmaker and creative director Evan Ezquer, the studio combines cutting-edge storytelling with hands-on production. Our capabilities include: • AI films and cinematic storytelling: Original short films, branded stories, commercials, and immersive visual worlds, with deliberate camera direction, emotional pacing, and sound design. • Microdramas and vertical series: Character-driven episodes built around compelling hooks, escalating conflict, and cliffhangers. We develop recurring characters and maintain consistent faces, costumes, environments, and visual identities across shots and episodes. • Social media advertising: Product-led and narrative ads for Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and Amazon, including animated concepts, realistic AI scenes, alternative opening hooks, and platform-specific cutdowns. We handle concept development, scripting, storyboards, character design, image and video generation, dialogue and lip-sync, editing, music, sound design, and final delivery. Our multi-model workflow draws on Seedance, Kling, Runway, Veo, Midjourney, and other tools, with post-production in Adobe Premiere Pro and Photoshop. Our portfolio includes work for GIGABYTE, Beardbrand, Pete & Pedro, Karviva, and Mad Viking Beard Co. Evan is a Project Odyssey winner for his horror short Cursed! and a Chroma Awards winner for Marketing Video for Beardbrand’s Zeus’s Beard Itch. Based in Cebu, Philippines, we collaborate remotely with clients worldwide, bringing a knack for Western humor and comedic timing to stories that connect across cultures.
