About

One-Sentence Summary Fluidfit.ai is a no-code AI visual automation platform that empowers brands, marketers, and creative teams to produce studio-quality images at scale — without expensive photoshoots or coding experience. AI Business Description Fluidfit.ai is reshaping how brands, marketers, and creatives build, scale, and deliver high-quality visual content. Built on state-of-the-art diffusion and generative AI, the platform empowers teams to automate repetitive visual tasks — from bulk background removal to on-model outfit changes — enabling them to focus on creativity and strategy rather than production overhead. Founded by Shikha Arora (CEO) and Greg Morrow (CRO), Fluidfit.ai is designed for creatives of all skill levels. No coding and no design experience is required. Users simply define their goal, upload their images or generate new ones from scratch, choose an AI operation, and apply changes instantly — making it one of the most accessible and flexible AI visual tools available for e-commerce, fashion, advertising, and social media. The platform's standout features include AI-powered face and body swap, outfit change, background removal and replacement, 3D conversion and 360° spin, inpainting and outpainting, and in-image text insertion. For teams with existing systems, Fluidfit.ai also offers a robust API and full documentation to integrate AI visual flows directly into current workflows. The platform serves a wide range of industries including e-commerce, fashion and retail, advertising, social media, print and publishing, automotive, and marketing agencies. Fluidfit.ai's vision is to revolutionize visual content creation by making high-quality, AI-driven design accessible, fast, and scalable for every creative team — reducing production costs and time to market by cutting expensive photoshoots altogether. Fluidfit.ai is a no-code AI automation platform for visual media. It enables brands, marketers, and creators to automate image editing at scale — from swapping outfits and removing backgrounds to generating 3D product spins and inserting text — all without photography or coding expertise. Who is a good fit to work with Fluidfit.ai? Fluidfit.ai is built for graphic designers, social media managers, digital marketers, photographers, content creators, e-commerce sellers, brand strategists, and advertising agencies. Any team that produces high volumes of visual content and wants to cut production time and costs is an ideal fit. What makes Fluidfit.ai's approach different? Fluidfit.ai combines the power of generative AI with a no-code, workflow-first interface. Rather than offering a single editing tool, it enables users to assemble entire visual pipelines — called Flows — that are reusable, clonable, and scalable. Teams can test new GenAI capabilities without learning a new tool or adding a new subscription. What results can users expect? Users can eliminate expensive photoshoots by generating on-model product imagery directly, reduce time to market significantly, and produce consistent, studio-quality assets across e-commerce listings, advertising campaigns, and social channels — all from a single platform.
