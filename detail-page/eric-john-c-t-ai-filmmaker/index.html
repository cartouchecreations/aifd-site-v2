About

I'm Eric John Côté, an AI filmmaker, creative director, editor, and screenwriter specializing in cinematic generative video, narrative storytelling, and end-to-end AI production. I work from concept and script through visual development, shot design, AI generation, directing, editing, VFX, sound design, and final delivery. I combine more than 20 years of traditional filmmaking, editing, motion design, advertising, and post-production experience with hands-on generative AI workflows. My current work includes agency AI production for Google, LG TV, and other enterprise clients, AI filmmaking and production consulting for MasterClass, branded content, narrative films, commercials, music-driven projects, and emerging formats such as microdramas. My focus is not just generating individual AI shots. I build sequences that work as films, with consistent characters, cinematic camera language, performance direction, pacing, visual continuity, sound, and storytelling. I also develop scripts, characters, worlds, dialogue, story structure, and previsualization for AI-native productions. My workflow includes tools and models such as Seedance, Kling, Veo, MiniMax, Runway, Midjourney, Nano Banana, FLUX, ComfyUI, ElevenLabs, Suno, Higgsfield, ChatGPT, Claude, Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, Photoshop, Blender, Topaz Video AI, and Magnific. I'm available for AI filmmaking, creative direction, generative video production, commercials, branded content, narrative projects, music videos, previsualization, post-production, and consulting. Portfolio and additional work can be found at Moldero.com.
