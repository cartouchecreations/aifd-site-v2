About

Multi-award-winning AI filmmaker from South Wales, UK I’m Shaun Preece, a multi-award-winning AI filmmaker and creative director from South Wales, and I make films that make people feel something. Emotion has always been the cornerstone of marketing for me, and storytelling is how you reach it. AI can copy a look in seconds, but it doesn’t feel a thing, so the heart of a film still has to come from the person making it. Every brief starts with one question: what should the audience feel? The script, the casting, the light, the music and the last frame all exist to get them there. I look for the small human moments that stay with people, like a kindness between strangers or a memory you didn’t expect to come back. I came to AI filmmaking after twenty years in brand and creative direction, working with The Royal Mint, Bentley and Sony, founding TH3M in 2016 and exiting companies I helped build. My campaign for The Royal Mint returned 1,100% ROI, so when I make a film for a brand, I know what it has to do commercially as well as emotionally. My debut short, Priority Seat, won Best Super Short Film at the Cannes World Film Festival and six more awards, including Best Director. I write and direct across drama, comedy and brand films, and I design every character, set and prop before I make a single shot. I’m fussy about the things that give AI away, so every performance has to feel real, right down to the smallest glance. And when the tool I need doesn’t exist, I build it.
