About

Bengal Stripe Films Telling Stories Through Motion Picture. Business Description Bengal Stripe Films is an internationally focused film production company that combines the foundations of traditional filmmaking with the creative possibilities of artificial intelligence. Building on a background in conventional film production, we specialise in AI-powered commercials, cinematic product films, brand content and original narrative projects. We bring together storytelling, creative direction and emerging technology to produce visually compelling, purposeful and professionally crafted films for a global audience. Mission To unite the craft and discipline of traditional filmmaking with the possibilities of AI to create compelling visual stories grounded in proven storytelling and production principles. Vision To become a recognised leader in AI-integrated filmmaking, distinguished by traditional filmmaking expertise, creative excellence and innovative visual storytelling. Values Storytelling First: Every film begins with a compelling idea and a clear purpose. Craft and Quality: We uphold the principles and disciplines of professional filmmaking. Innovation with Intention: We use technology to serve creativity, not replace it. Creative Integrity: We approach every project with originality, purpose and attention to detail. Continuous Evolution: We embrace new possibilities while respecting the foundations of our craft.
