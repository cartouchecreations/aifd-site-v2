About

UK-based AI filmmaker making vertical microdramas and episodic series with consistent characters and cliffhangers. Remote for studios and brands. I take a short-form story from idea to delivery: scriptwriting, visual development, AI video generation, editing, sound and final export. I bring about 15 years of writing, directing and producing, with training at Pinewood Studios, and I use AI to make cinematic work at vertical-series speed: a polished 60-second piece in 3-4 days. You can hire me for: - AI vertical drama series and microdramas for platforms and production companies - AI commercials and branded narrative content for agencies and brands - Story development, scripts and series bibles for AI-native projects Proof: Best Producer, We Make Filmmakers Festival 2025 (Children's Voice, short documentary). FORCED, a live-action short with two international festival selections, including BBB SFF Greece 2024. NOT EXORCISTS, a completed vertical microdrama produced for Big Screen Hack London. HOTEL MOORLAND, a vertical horror series built around recurring characters, cliffhangers and episodic progression. FIZZ, a 50-second AI commercial made for the Runway Big Ad Contest.
