About

Dear Filmakademie Team, My professional background combines photography, film and video production, animation, 3D, post-production and, in recent years, intensive work with generative AI. I originally started as a professional photographer and have more than 30 years of experience in photography, including product, staged and documentary/reportage photography. I later moved increasingly into video production and editing, which I have been doing professionally for more than 15 years. An important part of my background is 3D. I am a certified 3D Maya Artist and worked professionally as a 3D Artist for several years. I also worked as a 2D animator. Because of this background, I am very familiar with working not only with final images, but with composition, camera, perspective, lighting, materials, animation, continuity and the complete visual production pipeline. I started working with generative AI as soon as these tools became publicly available and have been testing and using different image and video models ever since. Today, AI is part of my daily professional work. I work with different models depending on the task, including Seedance, Kling, Veo, Runway, Higgsfield, MidJourney, Nano Banana and others. I also use WaveSpeed AI extensively for generation. For post-production and visual work, I regularly use Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe After Effects and Adobe Photoshop, as well as Topaz Video AI. My workflow normally includes much more than prompting. I work with reference images and video, model settings, multiple iterations, character and visual consistency, camera and movement control, editing, compositing, color correction, upscaling and final delivery. Depending on the project, I combine AI generation with traditional 2D and 3D methods. Last year I worked for eight months on a documentary project for Swiss television. One of my tasks was to create animated sequences based on real historical people: the characters had to remain recognizable from their real prototypes while being transformed into a consistent 2D visual style. This required working with references, maintaining character consistency and integrating the generated and animated material into a professional television production. I also have direct experience working according to prescribed evaluation protocols. From November 2023 to January 2025, I worked with Outlier on AI training and evaluation tasks, including prompt-related work and the evaluation of AI-generated content according to predefined criteria and structured workflows. This experience is directly relevant to systematic model testing, workflow transparency and reproducible evaluation processes. All examples you see in the links were created by me from beginning to end. I was responsible for the complete production process: the initial idea and concept, visual development, image generation, video generation, animation, editing, compositing, music, sound, color correction and final delivery. In other words, these are not isolated AI generations or individual production steps — I created each project from zero to the finished video. Because I work with AI tools every day, testing models and comparing different generation approaches is already a normal part of my workflow. I am also accustomed to documenting production steps and can work transparently with defined workflows, parameters, references and iterations. I speak German, English and Russian . I live and work in Switzerland and have a professional home studio. I would be very interested in participating in this project from November 2026 to May 2027. I can provide specific examples of AI film, television, advertising and animation work together with descriptions of the workflows, tools and the production steps I completed personally. Best regards, Nickola Pankov
