About

AI Dancemation® – Stories first. AI second. AI Dancemation® is an independent AI filmmaking and creative production initiative founded by Carsten Iversen. We develop and produce cinematic AI-driven films, original stories, branded content and short-form video experiences. Combining creative direction, narrative development and generative AI technologies, we create visually distinctive content with a strong focus on storytelling, atmosphere and character consistency. Our services include: AI filmmaking and generative video production Creative direction and visual concept development AI-powered storytelling and narrative development Character design and consistency Short-form video and micro-drama production Commercials and branded storytelling AI-assisted editing and post-production Our work ranges from cinematic science fiction and mystery projects to brand films and experimental visual storytelling. We combine human creative direction with AI-native production workflows to turn ideas into compelling visual experiences. Based in Germany. Available for international collaborations and remote projects. Portfolio: https://bit.ly/ai-dancemation
